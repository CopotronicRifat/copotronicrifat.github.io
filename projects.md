## Research and Projects

This archive retains the research and software work from my previous site. Current language-model research is presented first; earlier projects document the breadth of my technical development.

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Efficient and Controllable Language Models</h3>
  <p>Representation-guided token retention, input-conditioned low-rank routing, memory-efficient adaptation, symbolic planning, and causal intervention. See the <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publication list</a> for MaskLoRA, CCM-LoRA, controller modules, and C³T.</p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Estimating Influenza Cases with Numerical Methods and Machine Learning (2024)</h3>
  <p>Compared SVIR-based numerical solvers with LSTM, CNN, FTA-LSTM, and Random Forest models for H1N1 forecasting; Random Forest with direct forecasting achieved the best performance on the evaluated data.</p>
  <p><a class="resource-link" href="https://github.com/CopotronicRifat/Numerical-Methods-for-Estimating-Influenza-Cases" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub</a></p>
</div>

<div class="project-card">
  <h3>Adaptive Blockchain with Dynamic Difficulty and SJF Prioritization (2024)</h3>
  <p>Designed a blockchain simulation with dynamic difficulty adjustment and shortest-job-first transaction prioritization to improve throughput and reduce queueing under high loads.</p>
  <p><a class="resource-link" href="https://github.com/CopotronicRifat/Adaptive-Blockchain" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub</a></p>
</div>
