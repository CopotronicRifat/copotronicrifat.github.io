## Teaching Philosophy and Interests

I treat computing courses as a bridge between conceptual reasoning and careful implementation. My courses emphasize transparent problem-solving, reproducible experimentation, iterative feedback, and the ability to explain technical choices to different audiences. My teaching interests include **natural language processing, machine learning, deep learning, responsible AI, data mining, algorithms, and introductory programming**.
